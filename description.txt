剑之大师模组来袭！  
剑之大师模组将原版已有的一些剑类近战武器额外制作成了召唤师的御剑武器。  
御剑的攻击模式基于原版泰拉棱镜，而且效果更加多样！  
目前最高可合成到天顶剑，后续会继续更新更多样的御剑。

【目前内容】  
+ 70 物品  
+ 1 城镇 NPC（未完全）  
+ 18 武器前缀（可关闭）  
+ 友方弹幕透明度调节

【小贴士】  
• 剑鞘、军火库、魔法袋这类道具可以适应大部分武器；如有武器无法适配，请右键 Buff 取消掉召唤物。  
• 武器【魔剑】合成材料“魔石”获得方式为：永夜御刃微光分解。
• 剑鞘进行了除天顶剑、南瓜剑之外的原版适配，军火库、魔法袋并未进行原版适配，请见谅。
• 当弹药数量堆叠到3996时，军火库默认为不消耗弹药。
• 武器前缀与灾厄的取消随机重铸冲突，要体验模组前缀请先取消灾厄配置里的重铸修改。

「SwordMaster Mod – English Release Notes」  

“When you can command nearly every weapon to fight for you, how will it feel?”  

The SwordMaster Mod has arrived!  It takes many existing swords from vanilla Terraria and re-creates them as summonable “Flying Swords” for summoners.  
• Attack style: based on the Terra Prism, but with more varied effects.  
• Current top craft: Terra Blade – more swords are coming soon.

Content so far  
+ 70 items  
+ 1 town NPC (WIP)  
+ 18 weapon prefixes (toggleable)  
+ Friendly-projectile transparency slider  

Quick tips  
– Items like Scabbard, Armory, and Magic Bag can adapt most weapons. If a weapon refuses to adapt, right-click its buff to dismiss the summon.  
– Material “Demon Stone” for the Demonblade is obtained by shimmer-decomposing the Night’s Edge summon version.
– Scabbard now supports all vanilla melee weapons except Zenith and The Horseman’s Blade. Armory and Magic Bag have **not** been adapted for vanilla gear—sorry for the inconvenience.  
– When ammo stacks reach 3,996, Armory defaults to **no ammo consumption**.  
– Weapon prefixes conflict with Calamity’s “disable random reforge.” To use this mod’s prefixes, first turn off Calamity’s reforge tweaks in its config.

Enjoy the mod!  Leave feedback or bug reports below.  If you like it, give us a thumbs-up!
